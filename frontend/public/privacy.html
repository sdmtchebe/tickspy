Privacy and Cookie Policy

In short: TickSPY is operated by Tickspycorp, Québec, Canada. No account is required. Preferences and optional Alpaca keys remain in your browser. Support messages reach our inbox. Hosting and resource providers receive normal web-request information. Advertising is disabled in this release.

Information this site handles

When you contact us, FormSubmit relays your name, email address, message, and optional screenshot to tickspysupport@gmail.com, hosted by Google Gmail. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. Sending a message is optional; please exclude API keys, account numbers, and sensitive financial information.

The desk stores your watchlist, alerts, preferences, and any optional Alpaca API keys in your browser's local storage. Those values are not stored by TickSPY. Alpaca keys are sent only to Alpaca when you choose to connect live data.

Google AdSense and cookies

No advertising runtime or advertising cookies are loaded in the current release. If a future deployment enables Google AdSense, the following choices apply. Enabling advertising requires an updated deployment and any consent platform required by Google and applicable law.

Advertising is optional. Until you choose Allow ads, the landing page does not load the Google AdSense runtime or request an ad. If you choose No thanks, no AdSense ad units are requested for that browser.

If you allow ads, this site asks Google for non-personalized ads. Google and its vendors may nevertheless use cookies, local storage, IP address, browser and device data, and similar technologies for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, security, and aggregated measurement. Google controls those technologies and may process data under its own policies.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and advertising technology information for details. There is no advertising choice to change in this release because advertising is disabled.

Service providers

Netlify hosts the website and proxies public edge requests to Cloudflare. Both process request information such as IP addresses, requested paths, timestamps, and browser details and may retain operational or security logs according to their own policies. Tickspycorp does not operate an account database or independent analytics system.

Google Fonts receives your IP address and browser details when typefaces load. jsDelivr provides TensorFlow.js when the volatility model runs. The model trains in your browser; your credentials and watchlist are not sent to jsDelivr.

With your own credentials, your browser contacts Alpaca directly for data and streams; Tickspycorp's edge proxy does not receive those credentials. Without credentials, the desk loads the previous completed session from HF Data Library (IEX exchange data), headlines from public feeds, and calendar entries from FairEconomy. Those upstream requests carry the server's network address, not your browser's. Google Gemini receives public headlines and calendar information from the edge server to produce a shared overview; no user-entered prompts or credentials are sent.

FormSubmit and Gmail process submitted messages. Providers can process and store information outside Québec and Canada, including in the United States. Their privacy practices and lawful access rules may differ from those in Québec.

Retention and choices

Your advertising choice, if made, is saved under tickspy-ad-consent. Desk storage includes cfg (optional Alpaca keys), wl (watchlist), alerts, calalert, calfired, calc (calendar cache), legal (disclosure acceptance), and wiz (setup preference). Storage remains until you clear this site's browser data or remove the relevant item in the desk. Browser-stored keys are not encrypted by TickSPY. Use Paper-account keys and avoid shared devices. If storage is unavailable, preferences are held only for the current page session.

Support messages are kept only as needed to answer the request, resolve the issue, and meet applicable legal obligations. Request deletion through the support address. Third-party log and backup retention follows each provider's own practices.

Your rights and privacy contact

You may request access to or correction of personal information held by Tickspycorp, request deletion where applicable, withdraw consent to optional processing, or raise a privacy complaint at tickspysupport@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing already lawfully completed. We may need proportionate verification before providing private correspondence.

Privacy requests are directed to the person exercising the highest authority at Tickspycorp, responsible for personal-information protection unless that responsibility is delegated. The same email is the published privacy contact. You can also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

Eligibility and security

The desk is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information. If such information was submitted, contact us to request its removal. HTTPS and browser security controls reduce risks, but no website or browser storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Contact and changes

For support and privacy questions, email tickspysupport@gmail.com. This policy may change when site functionality or providers change; this page will show the updated date.
